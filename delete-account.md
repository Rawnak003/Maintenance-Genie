# Delete Your Maintenance Genie Account

**Maintenance Genie**, developed by Big Lick Enterprises

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Maintenance Genie account and all associated data in either of the following ways:

### Option 1: Delete directly within the app
1. Open the **Maintenance Genie** app
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

### Option 2: Request deletion by email
If you're unable to access the app, send an email to **admin@biglickenterprises.com** with:
- Subject line: **"Account Deletion Request"**
- The email address associated with your account

We will verify your identity and process your request within **30 days**.

---

## What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

- Your name, email address, and phone number
- Asset data (vehicle, home, or equipment details you entered)
- Photos you uploaded
- Usage data and preferences associated with your account

---

## What Data May Be Retained

We may retain limited information for a longer period where required by law, such as for tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. This data is deleted or anonymized once it is no longer legally required to be kept.
