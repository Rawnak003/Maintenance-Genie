# Delete Your Maintenance Genie Account

**Maintenance Genie**, developed by Big Lick Enterprises

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your Maintenance Genie account and all associated data directly within the app:

1. Open the **Maintenance Genie** app
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

We will verify your identity and process your request within **30 days**.

---

## Changed Your Mind?

Logging in to your account during the 30-day period cancels the deletion request and restores your account. After 30 days, your account and associated data are permanently deleted.

---

## What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

- Your name, email address, and address
- Your profile picture
- Asset data (vehicle, home, or equipment details you entered)
- Tasks and maintenance history
- Photos and receipts you uploaded
- Notification history and your device's push notification token
- Usage data and preferences associated with your account

---

## What Data May Be Retained

We may retain limited information for a longer period where required by law, such as for tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. This data is deleted or anonymized once it is no longer legally required to be kept.

---

## Your Subscription

Deleting your account does **not** cancel an active premium subscription. To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription through your app store account settings (Google Play or the Apple App Store).
